2026 Black Friday Shopping Calendar & Planning Guide

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday falls on Monday, November 30. Use this calendar to research prices early, set a budget and focus on purchases you actually need.

August–September: Build your plan

Make a list of planned purchases and set a realistic budget for each category. Note the products that matter most so you can research them before the busiest sale period.

October: Research normal prices

Track the usual prices of important items and compare exact models, sizes and versions. This gives you useful context when retailers publish sale prices.

Early November: Organize your shortlist

Review your list, remove purchases you no longer need and identify trusted stores or suitable alternatives. Check return policies and delivery expectations before the major sale weekend.

Black Friday — November 27, 2026

Compare the actual checkout price rather than the advertised percentage alone. Include shipping, taxes, membership requirements and any conditions attached to a promotion.

Cyber Monday — November 30, 2026

Compare online offers for the same products or suitable alternatives. Do not rush into a purchase simply because a countdown or limited-time message creates pressure.

December: Finish with a clear budget

After Cyber Monday, focus on remaining needs and delivery deadlines. Keep order confirmations and review return windows for important purchases.
